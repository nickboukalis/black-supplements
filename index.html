Questions

Asked & answered

Why is the drink black?+

Activated charcoal powder — 250mg per scoop. It's a real ingredient in the formula, not a dye. When it hits water it disperses and turns the entire drink jet black. It's our signature, and it's how you know it's ours.

How much caffeine is in a scoop?+

200mg per serving — roughly two cups of coffee. Enough to drive a serious session without leaving you wired at midnight. We recommend starting with half a scoop if you're sensitive to caffeine.

How does Subscribe & Save work?+

Pick "Subscribe monthly" on any product and you save 10% on every order. A fresh tub ships every 30 days automatically, and you can pause or cancel anytime — no lock-in, no hoops.

What does shipping cost?+

Standard shipping is $6.99 flat, or free on orders over $50 — that's a pre-workout plus a creatine, or any two tubs. Expedited shipping is available at checkout.

Anything I should know about activated charcoal?+

Charcoal can bind to things it shares a stomach with — so if you take any medications, keep a couple of hours between them and your scoop, and check with your doctor if you're unsure.

How do I take the creatine?+

One 5g scoop daily, any time of day — consistency matters more than timing. It's unflavored and micronized, so it dissolves into water, juice, a shake, or straight into your pre-workout.

Who makes this?+

Two co-founders who wanted a pre-workout they'd actually take every day — so they built one, tested it on their own training, and put every dose on the front of the tub.
